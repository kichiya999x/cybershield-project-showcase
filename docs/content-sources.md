# Content Sources

## Primary research source

The project content was checked against the private CyberShield thesis supplied to the project team. The thesis is not published by this repository. The public Research Summary paraphrases verified findings and does not reproduce the full document.

Verified source material used in the site includes:

- research title, authors, adviser, institution, background, objectives, scope, and methodology;
- the four role-based dashboards and functional areas described in Chapters 3 and 4;
- Developmental Research, DevSecOps, ISO/IEC 25010, STRIDE, and OWASP ASVS;
- the 100-participant quality evaluation;
- the reported 4.73 overall composite mean and characteristic means of 4.94, 4.78, 4.76, 4.70, and 4.49;
- 24 application-layer security tests: 21 full passes, one pass with a note, and two remediation findings;
- the reported 87.50% full-pass rate and 91.67% accepted-outcome rate;
- the account-lockout and malformed numeric-input remediation findings; and
- the need for separate validation of direct media access, broader MFA coverage, AES-256 at-rest protection, and deployment-level WAF/SIEM controls.

## Public wording rules

- Describe CyberShield as an academic prototype evaluated in a controlled environment.
- Do not describe it as a live NAPOLCOM platform or a production security certification.
- Distinguish participant quality ratings from technical security-test results.
- Use the disclosure “Interface shown with non-operational demonstration data.” with interface images.
- Treat WAF and SIEM as planned or deployment-level controls, not verified application features.
